What we recover

Marine upholstery done right — plus the custom work.

Boats are our lane, from a single livewell seat to a full deck redesign — and we handle furniture cushion covers for the home, too. If it has a seat, a cushion or a cover, bring it to us.

Boat seats & helm chairs

Leaning posts, helm seats and full deck sets rebuilt in marine-grade vinyl that stands up to Florida sun, salt and water — better than new when they leave.

Bolsters & side panels

Rub rails, gunwale bolsters and side panels wrapped so they hug every curve of the hull — the detail that makes a re-do look factory, not patched.

Livewell & console covers

New vinyl for livewell seats, center-console backrests and helm pads — the high-wear pieces that take the sun, priced fair with quick turnaround.

Cushions & bow lounges

Bow lounges, cabin settees and cockpit cushions rebuilt with fresh foam and new covers — comfort and shape restored, not just the fabric.

Furniture cushion covers

Zippered cushion covers and sofa cushions for the home, sewn to fit and finished beautifully — prompt, fair and a pleasure to work with.

Design, foam & repairs

New foam, restitched seams and a hands-on design process — Crystal helps you pick colors and a scheme that flows through the whole vessel.

On the water

Marine work built to take the Florida sun.

Sun and salt are hard on a boat — worn, cracked, sun-beaten vinyl doesn't last. We rebuild helm seats, bolsters, livewell seats and full deck sets in marine-grade materials so the interior looks sharp and holds up season after season.

From center consoles to offshore boats like the Grady White Canyon and Intrepid, the finish comes back tight, clean and better than new — including the diamond stitch, matched piping and bolster wrap that set a real upholstery shop apart.

  • Helm seats, leaning posts, bolsters & full deck sets
  • Marine-grade vinyl and thread that resist sun, salt & mildew
  • Good pricing and turnaround better than most owners expect
